The "what kind of business" question in Stripe KYC

For student artists, they should select Individual / Sole Proprietor, not any business type. It just asks for their name, address, SSN last 4, and date of birth. That's it. No business registration, no EIN, nothing complicated. The form looks scarier than it is.

Minors — this is the more serious issue

Yes, this is a genuine legal and practical problem. A few things to know:

Stripe's policy: Stripe requires account holders to be 18 or older. A 16-year-old cannot legally create their own Stripe Express account. If a minor tries and enters their real birthdate, Stripe will reject them.

Contracts: Minors cannot enter into binding contracts in most US states, which means their agreement to Stripe's TOS and potentially to your seller terms is legally unenforceable without parental consent.

What this means for BrushBids: If a significant portion of your artists are under 18, Option A from my last message isn't just a convenience — it's basically a legal necessity in the near term. You'd collect a parent's PayPal or bank details for payouts instead, and parents sign off on their child selling on the platform.

What you should actually do:

During artist onboarding, add an age gate. If the artist is under 18, require a parent/guardian email and have them agree to the seller terms on the minor's behalf. Payouts go to the parent's PayPal/Venmo. This is clean, defensible, and actually a selling point to parents who are protective of their kids online.

Bottom line: The manual payout route (Option A) isn't just simpler — for a platform built around student artists, many of whom may be minors, it's the right call legally until you're at a scale where the legal infrastructure to handle minor accounts properly is worth building out. Good thinking. Here's how that should work end to end:

The flow:

When an artist registers, they enter their birthdate. The system stores it and checks it on every login (or daily in the background). The moment they turn 18, their account automatically upgrades — they get a notification saying they can now set up direct Stripe payouts if they want, and the payout method field opens up to include that option alongside PayPal/Venmo.

Nothing breaks for them in the meantime. While under 18, payouts go to the parent PayPal/Venmo on file. On their 18th birthday (or the next time they log in after it), the system recognizes the change and prompts them to optionally upgrade.
