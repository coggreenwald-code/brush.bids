Completely redesign the BrushBids homepage. The design should fuse Cyrculo's dark, fluid, scroll-driven aesthetic with Omnera's editorial precision and intentional polish. The result should feel like a premium digital gallery — immersive, dark, and alive with motion, but never cluttered. Every animation should feel deliberate. Every element should earn its place.
Reference sites for design direction:

Cyrculo (https://cyrclo.webflow.io/) — dark theme, ✦ markers, image grids, rotating text, fluid scroll animations, service cards with icon badges
Omnera (https://omnerat-template.webflow.io/) — editorial clarity, large showcase images, custom cursor with crosshair lines, refined intentional motion, clean structure

GLOBAL DESIGN ELEMENTS (apply across entire page)
Custom cursor (inspired by Omnera)
Replace the default cursor sitewide with a custom cursor:

A small circle (20px diameter, 1px solid rgba(240,240,246,0.5), no fill)
With crosshair lines extending outward: 4 thin lines (1px, 12px long) radiating from the circle at 0°, 90°, 180°, 270° — forming a + crosshair through the circle
The circle + crosshair follows the mouse with slight easing/lag (use requestAnimationFrame with lerp, ~0.15 factor)
On hover over interactive elements (buttons, cards, links): circle expands to 48px, lines fade out, circle gets a subtle --accent fill at 15% opacity
On click: quick scale pulse (1.0 to 0.85 to 1.0 over 150ms)
Hide the native cursor with cursor: none on body
On mobile/touch: disable entirely, show normal cursor

✦ Decorative markers (from Cyrculo)
Use the ✦ character (or similar: ◆, ✧) as decorative accents throughout:

Between nav links: "Auctions ✦ How It Works ✦ Artists"
In section eyebrows: "✦ ENDING SOON"
As list markers in feature descriptions
In the ticker strip between items
Color: --accent or --accent2, never white or gray

Scroll-triggered reveals (from both)
Every section and its children animate in on scroll:

Use Framer Motion whileInView
Default animation: opacity 0→1, y: 50→0, duration 0.7s, ease [0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1]
Stagger children by 120ms
Images additionally scale from 1.05→1.0
Ensure elements only animate ONCE (viewport: { once: true })

Thin accent lines (from Omnera)
Use 1px horizontal and vertical lines as structural separators:

Horizontal lines above section headers (full-width, --border color)
Vertical accent lines alongside key content (2px wide, --accent, partial height)
These lines should animate in: grow from 0 width/height to full with 0.6s ease
